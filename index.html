Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 20-Jul-2026  |  Last Updated: 20-Jul-2026

This privacy policy applies to the FinTrack app (hereby referred to as "Application") for mobile devices that was created by AgasteeInDev (hereby referred to as "Service Provider") as an Ad Supported service. This service is intended for use "AS IS".

1. Information We Collect

The Service Provider does not collect, transmit, or store any of your personal or financial data on any server. All the financial data you enter — assets, liabilities, transactions, and notes — is stored locally on your device only, encrypted with a password that only you know. The Service Provider has no access to this data and cannot recover it if you forget your password or uninstall the app.

However, third-party services integrated into the Application may collect certain limited information:

a. AdMob (Google Ads)

AdMob may collect data such as:

  • Device identifiers (Advertising ID)
  • IP address
  • App usage statistics

For more details, visit the Google AdMob Privacy Policy.

b. Currency Exchange Rate Service (Frankfurter API)

To convert between currencies, the Application sends currency codes (e.g., "USD", "EUR") to the Frankfurter exchange rate API (api.frankfurter.dev). No personal or financial data, account details, or device identifiers are sent as part of this request — only the currency codes needed to fetch exchange rates.

2. How We Use Collected Information

The data collected by third-party services is used for:

  • Ad Personalization (AdMob)
  • Currency conversion (Frankfurter API)

3. Biometric Authentication

The Application offers an optional biometric unlock feature (fingerprint/face recognition) using your device's built-in biometric hardware. This authentication is handled entirely by your device's operating system. The Service Provider does not receive, store, or have any access to your biometric data (fingerprint, face scan, etc.) at any point. Biometric data never leaves your device.

4. Data Storage & Backups

All your financial data is stored locally on your device in encrypted form. You may optionally export an encrypted backup file to save or transfer your data. These backup files are encrypted and remain under your control — the Service Provider never receives or has access to them. It is your responsibility to store backup files securely, as anyone with the backup file and your password could access the data within it.

5. Third-Party Services

The Application may contain links to third-party services (such as ad content or exchange rate providers). The Service Provider is not responsible for the privacy practices of these third parties. Please review their respective privacy policies before proceeding.

6. Permissions

The Application may request the following permissions:

  • Internet Access — required to display ads and fetch live currency exchange rates.
  • Biometric/Fingerprint Hardware Access — used solely to unlock the app using your device's existing biometric authentication. No biometric data is captured, stored, or transmitted by the Application.

7. In-App Purchases

The Application offers an optional one-time purchase to remove ads and support development. Purchases are processed entirely through the Google Play Billing system. The Service Provider does not collect or store any payment information; this is handled by Google Play.

8. Data Retention

The Service Provider does not retain any user data, as none is transmitted or stored outside your device. Any data collected by third-party services (such as AdMob) is subject to their own retention policies. Please refer to Google's privacy policy for details.

9. Children's Privacy

This App is not intended for children under the age of 13. The Service Provider does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information through this Application, please contact the Service Provider so appropriate action can be taken.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. The Service Provider will notify you of any changes by posting the new privacy policy on this page with an updated effective date. You are advised to review this privacy policy periodically for any changes.
